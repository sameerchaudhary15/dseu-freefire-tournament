Rules

Tournament Rules

Keep it fair, keep it clean. Read every rule before registering.

All players must be current DSEU students with a valid Student ID.
Every player needs a real, personal Free Fire UID — no fake or duplicate IDs.
Emulators, hacks, modded APKs and auto-clickers are strictly banned.
Teams must enter custom lobby rooms before the 10-minute check-in window.
Late arrival or a missing player after check-in = straight disqualification.
The captain is responsible for sharing match details with their own squad.
One team per player. Playing for two teams = both teams are disqualified.
Entry fee is non-refundable once the match list for your slot is finalised.
Organisers' decisions on penalties & disqualifications are final.
Respect other players. Abusive or toxic behaviour leads to bans.
Heads up: slot limits apply. Roster changes are allowed only before the verification step is completed.
